The Technology: Fuzzy Logic Explained

Traditional metrics are binary and precise. Inflation, however, is a subjective experience. Our core innovation is the use of Fuzzy Logic.

"Instead of a hard 'Yes' or 'No,' Fuzzy Logic allows for 'Maybe' or 'A little bit.' This is essential for capturing the nuanced experience of cost-of-living changes."

The system converts your 0-10 slider inputs (e.g., "Food Cost Change is 8") and your text descriptions into fuzzy sets (Low, Medium, High). It then uses a robust rule-base to defuzzify this data, yielding the precise [0-20] Personal Inflation Score.
